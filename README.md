A cliff-rebound paper trading bot for the Pump.fun Replay Sprint: it trades 10 SOL of paper money on a 24h pump.fun replay. It buys a coin after a sharp 37% cliff that is already bouncing at least 2% off its low on positive net flow, and exits on take-profit (+30%), stop-loss (-10%), a trailing stop, or a 300-slot time stop. Paper money only — nothing is traded for real.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
